**__Version 1.5.0__**
- Every regiment now has its own uniforms
- Mexican line regiments each wear their own 1839 coatee (own lapel/collar colours, regiment number on the collar)
- All four Ligeros in the grey light infantry dress with scarlet-striped grey trousers
- Activos and San Patricio in the militia's red facings, batteries in the artillery coatee
- Mexican drummers and buglers wear green coats with their regiment's facings
- Mexican officers wear their regiment's coatee, silver epaulettes for cavalry, silver-strapped for militia
- New Mexican shakos: regiment number plates, activo name plates (VICTORIA, BRAVOS etc.), grenadier and light company shakos, officers' gold cords and scalloped chin scales
- US regulars in the sky-blue fatigue jacket (plus a white cotton summer jacket), officers in the 1847 shoulder straps and white-striped sky-blue trousers
- 2nd Dragoons: dragoon jackets, yellow-band caps, yellow-striped trousers, orange sash, gold-laced officers' jacket
- Mounted Riflemen no longer look like dragoons; Texans in buckskin hunting shirts and civilian hats; Indiana in the jeans hunter's frock coat; 1st Mississippi in red shirts and slouch hats
- Regiment selection portraits updated for the new uniforms

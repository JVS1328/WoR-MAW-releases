MAW 1.5.0

- Every regiment now dresses as its own unit, online too. You can still
  join normal servers; players without the mod see the vanilla game.
- Mexico: each line regiment wears its own 1839 coatee, with its own
  lapel and collar colours and its number on the collar. The four light
  battalions wear the grey light dress with grey trousers striped
  scarlet. The active militia battalions and San Patricio wear the
  militia's red facings, every battery the artillery coatee.
- Mexican drummers and buglers wear a green coat with their regiment's
  facings.
- Mexican officers wear their regiment's coatee, with silver epaulettes
  for the cavalry and silver-strapped ones for the militia.
- New Mexican shakos: each line regiment's plate with its number, the
  militia battalions' oval name plates, and grenadier (red band, grenade)
  and light company (bugle, green pompom) shakos. Officers have the gold
  cord loop and scalloped chin scales.
- United States: the regulars fight in the sky-blue fatigue jacket (with
  a white cotton summer jacket), and officers wear the 1847 shoulder
  straps and sky-blue trousers with the white stripe.
- 2nd Dragoons: dragoon jackets, yellow-banded caps, yellow-striped
  trousers, an orange sash and a gold-laced jacket for officers.
- The Mounted Riflemen have their own trousers and yellow chevrons, and
  no longer look like dragoons. The Texans wear buckskin hunting shirts,
  roundabouts and civilian hats. Indiana wears the jeans hunter's frock
  coat, the 1st Mississippi red shirts and slouch hats.
- The regiment selection portraits show the new uniforms. The Mexican
  portraits now come in two files (z_maw_UI_Textures_CSA.pak and
  z_maw_UI_Textures_CSA_2.pak).
